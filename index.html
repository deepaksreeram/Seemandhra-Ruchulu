Our Heritage & Story

At Seemandhra Ruchulu, we are dedicated to serving authentic Andhra home-style tiffins and delicacies right in the heart of Hyderabad. Our recipes are passed down through generations, capturing the true essence of traditional village kitchens.

We believe in "100% Home Style Taste" — using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients, stone-ground spices, and traditional iron-griddle cooking methods without any artificial colors or preservatives.
