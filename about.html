Our Story & Values

At Seemandhra Ruchulu, we are committed to bringing the warm, comforting flavors of traditional Andhra home cooking to food lovers in Hyderabad. Founded on the principle of uncompromised authenticity, our menu features beloved regional recipes prepared just like in our home kitchens.

From our flagship Uggani Bajji and crispy Gunta Ponganalu to healthy Jonna Rotelu and daily millet specials, every dish is crafted fresh daily using stone-ground spices, pure ghee, and unadulterated ingredients.
